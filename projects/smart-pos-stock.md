# Smart POS & Stock

## Retail Point-of-Sale & Inventory System

A commercial-ready POS and stock-management project focused on fast checkout, product control, billing and practical day-to-day retail operations.

### Highlights

- Fast POS checkout workflow
- Product and inventory management
- Stock-aware sales
- Discounts and pricing workflow
- Customer-ready invoices and receipts
- Thermal-print workflow
- Sales and inventory visibility
- Responsive interface
- Desktop-oriented business workflow

### Project Type

Commercial/client-ready retail software.
