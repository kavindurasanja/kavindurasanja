# Repair PRO

## Mobile Repair Shop Management System

Repair PRO is a desktop business-management project designed around the day-to-day workflow of a mobile-phone repair shop. The goal is to keep repair tickets, customers, technicians, payments, printing, and branch operations in one practical system.

### Highlights

- Repair ticket lifecycle and status history
- Customer, device, IMEI and problem tracking
- Technician assignment and work-status workflow
- Advance payments and balance tracking
- Invoice and job-ticket printing
- QR-based ticket lookup workflow
- Warranty selection and invoice presentation
- Reports and Excel export workflows
- Multi-branch ticket sharing and transfer workflows
- Firebase-connected real-time architecture
- SMS integration workflow through Cloudflare Workers / Notify.lk

### Technology

Electron · JavaScript · Firebase / Firestore · ExcelJS · Cloudflare Workers

### Project Type

Commercial/private-source business software.
