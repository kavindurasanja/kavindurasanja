# StudyFlow LK

## Student Time-Management Android App

StudyFlow LK is an Android productivity project for O/L, A/L and university students, designed around planning, focus sessions and study-progress tracking.

### Highlights

- Subject timetable
- Tuition timetable
- Exam countdowns
- Daily study targets
- Pomodoro timer and stopwatch
- Study-time tracking by subject
- Progress charts
- Past-paper tracking
- Revision planning
- Homework deadlines
- Smart reminders
- Weekly repeat workflows
- Streaks, XP, levels and badges
- Offline-first usage
- Sinhala and English support

### Project Type

Android productivity application project.

> This page documents the product concept and implementation work. Distribution builds are handled separately.
