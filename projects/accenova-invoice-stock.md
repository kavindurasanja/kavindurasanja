# ACCENOVA Invoice & Stock System

## Commercial Invoice & Inventory Management Software

A client-ready invoice and stock-management system designed for a mobile-accessories business, with clean billing, real-time inventory tracking and professional document output.

### Highlights

- Product and stock management
- Real-time inventory updates
- Professional A4 invoice output
- Serial number tracking per item
- Warranty details per product
- Payment methods including KOKO, MINTPAY and PAYZY
- Invoice summaries and customer-ready billing
- Delete confirmation and safer data actions
- Responsive business interface
- Clean branded document presentation

### Project Type

Commercial client software / private-source business system.

> Portfolio documentation only. Production source code and client-specific configuration are kept private.
